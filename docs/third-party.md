# Third-party dependencies

JUCE is pinned to 8.0.12, matching Meter, and is fetched at configure time unless `JUCE_SOURCE_DIR` points to a local checkout. JUCE and its embedded dependencies retain their own licence terms; review the checkout's `LICENSE.md` and dependency notices before distribution. No JUCE licence entitlement is implied by this scaffold.

`assets/DJLLogoWhite.png` is the existing DJL branding asset copied from Meter.
